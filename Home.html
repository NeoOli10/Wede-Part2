Welcome to a World of Sweetness & Nostalgia

Handcrafted candies made with the finest ingredients — from tangy sour sweets to delightful liquorice sticks and modern confections.

Our Story

Founded in 2022 by Katli Oliphant, our home-based venture quickly grew into a beloved candy shop in Johannesburg, serving sweet lovers across South Africa.

Our Mission

“To spread happiness, one sweet at a time by creating irresistible, high-quality candies that delight our customers and celebrate the joy of life.”

Our Vision

“To be South Africa’s most loved and trusted candy shop, known for innovation, quality, and bringing communities together through the universal language of sweetness.”

Core Values

  • Quality First: Finest ingredients for every treat.
  • Local Love: Support local suppliers & artisans.
  • Joy & Nostalgia: Sweet memories in every bite.
  • Innovation: Blend tradition with creativity.
  • Community: Bring people together through sweetness.

Our Treats
