Our Story

Founded in 2022 by Katli Oliphant, Katli’s Candy Shop began as a home-based venture selling handmade sour sweets and liquorice sticks at local markets. With unique flavors and colorful presentation, the shop quickly grew into a beloved candy shop in Johannesburg and online across South Africa.

Our Mission

“To spread happiness, one sweet at a time by creating irresistible, high-quality candies that delight our customers and celebrate the joy of life.”

Our Vision

“To be South Africa’s most loved and trusted candy shop, known for innovation, quality, and bringing communities together through the universal language of sweetness.”

Our Core Values

  • Quality First: Using only the finest ingredients for every treat.
  • Local Love: Supporting local suppliers and artisans.
  • Joy & Nostalgia: Creating experiences that bring back sweet childhood memories.
  • Innovation: Blending tradition with creativity to craft new favorites.
  • Community: Bringing people together through shared sweetness.
